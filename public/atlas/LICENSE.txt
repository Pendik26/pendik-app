Latin names
  latin.json holds the Latin (Terminologia Anatomica) name of each structure,
  group and landmark, from Z-Anatomy's translation table.

Descriptions
  The structure descriptions are Z-Anatomy's, which follow Wikipedia articles
  (CC BY-SA). They have been shortened to their first paragraphs.

Changes made here
  The Z-Anatomy FBX models (PC-Version branch) were converted to glTF with
  Blender 4.2, one file per body system plus the muscle attachment areas; label
  markers were removed; meshes were simplified (meshoptimizer, up to half the
  triangles), quantized, compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression) and gzipped. Colours are
  assigned by the app from the models' material names. The conversion is
  described in docs/atlas-3d.md in this repository.
