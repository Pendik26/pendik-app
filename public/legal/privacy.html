Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version. Pendik is the study app for the Pendik 26 class. Only students on the class roster can sign in. We store what's needed to sign you in and to keep your study progress and exam attempts. No ads, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Who runs it

Pendik is run by the class for the class. Its source code is at github.com/Pendik26/pendik-app. In this policy "we" means the class admins who run it.

What we store

  • The class roster: student id (NIM), full name, class group and cohort, added by an admin.
  • Your account: a sign-in made from your NIM, a password stored only as a salted hash, and, if you link it, your Google account's email and id.
  • Your progress: flashcard reviews, quiz results, reading positions, your exam plan, and your past-paper attempts with their answers and scores.
  • The leaderboard, only if you join it: a display name you choose, your cohort and your points. Your real name and answers are never shown to other students.
  • AI questions: what you ask Alfond or the explain button is sent to the AI provider to answer it, along with the page text when you choose to include it. We keep a count of how many you ask each day, not the questions.

Your browser also keeps device settings (theme, language, sidebar, recent pages). Those stay on your device.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Vercel serves the app's pages.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you use it, and hosts the class Drive files the app links to.
  • The AI provider configured for Alfond receives the questions you send it.

Pendik's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Leave the leaderboard at any time from the Leaderboard page.
  • Unlink Google from your Account page, or remove Pendik's access in your Google account settings.
  • Get a copy or have your data deleted: ask a class admin.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database only lets each student read and change their own progress; exam answer keys never reach the browser.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: ask a class admin, or open an issue at github.com/Pendik26/pendik-app/issues without personal details.
