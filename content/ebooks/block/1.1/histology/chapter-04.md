identification questions: telling the epithelium types apart, and
telling the blood cells apart. Use these side by side as a fast visual
reference while you work through the flashcards and quiz.

## Key teaching point

Both diagrams above answer the same style of question two different
ways: "what is this, and how do I know at a glance?" For epithelium, the
answer is layers + surface shape. For blood cells, it's size + nuclear
shape. Drill those two decision trees and most identification questions
in this subject become fast pattern matching instead of guesswork.
