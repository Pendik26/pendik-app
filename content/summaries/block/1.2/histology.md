# Histology: Muscle & Integument

## Muscle Tissue Basics

- **Muscle tissue**: muscle cells packed with contractile filaments (actin =
  thin, myosin = thick).
- **Classification**: histologically **striated** (skeletal, cardiac) vs
  **non-striated** (smooth); physiologically **voluntary** (skeletal) vs
  **involuntary** (cardiac, smooth). Cardiac = striated but involuntary.
- **Terms**: sarcoplasm = cytoplasm; sarcoplasmic reticulum = ER (Ca²⁺ store);
  **sarcosome = mitochondrion**; sarcolemma = cell membrane;
  **myofiber = muscle cell**; **myofibril = subunit of the cell** (chain of
  sarcomeres).
- **Section planes**: longitudinal (*membujur*) = long strips; transverse
  (*melintang*) = round islands. In the atlas figure
  **OL = transverse, IC = longitudinal**.

## Smooth Muscle (*otot polos*)

- **Fusiform** cells, **no striations**, one **elongated central nucleus**;
  wrapped in endomysium. Also called visceral muscle.
- **Where**: blood vessels, GI tract, respiratory tract, uterus, urinary
  bladder, arrector pili.
- **Control**: **autonomic** nervous system; the myoneural junction is a
  **synapse**. Slow, involuntary.
- **Cross-section**: profiles of different sizes; only the large ones contain a
  (central) nucleus.
- **Regeneration**: good (cells divide by **mitosis**).

## Skeletal Muscle (*otot rangka / lurik*)

- **Cylindrical, multinucleated** fibers;
  **oval nuclei peripheral, beneath the sarcolemma**; clear cross-striations.
  Voluntary.
- **Control**: **acetylcholine** at the **motor end plate** / neuromuscular
  junction. Location: limbs, trunk.
- **Sheaths**: **epimysium** (whole muscle, dense irregular CT) → **perimysium**
  (fascicle, dense irregular CT) → **endomysium** (each fiber, reticular
  fibers).
- **Transverse section**: nuclei at the edge; myofibril bundles appear as
  islands = **Cohnheim's areas**.
- **Longitudinal section**: **A band** dark (anisotropic, myosin); **I band**
  light (isotropic, actin). **Z line** in the middle of I; **M line** in the
  middle of A; **H zone** = light center of A. **Z to Z = sarcomere**.
- **Regeneration**: limited (satellite cells).

## Cardiac Muscle (*otot jantung*)

- Striated, **branching and anastomosing** fibers (*anastomose*),
  **intercalated discs** (irregular dark transverse lines between cells),
  **1 (sometimes 2) large oval central nucleus**.
- Involuntary; heart only. **No regeneration** after injury (scar).
- **vs skeletal**: central nuclei, branches, discs. **vs smooth**: striations
  and discs.

## Skin and Epidermis

- **Layers**: epidermis (**ectoderm**) → dermis (**mesoderm**) → hypodermis
  (subcutis). Epidermal ridges interlock with dermal papillae.
- **Epidermis**: **keratinized stratified squamous epithelium**, avascular,
  **nourished by diffusion** from the dermis.
- **Stratum basale**: one layer of cuboidal/columnar basophilic cells;
  **hemidesmosomes** to the basal lamina, desmosomes between cells;
  **mitotic stem cells**.
- **Stratum spinosum**: polygonal cells with "spines" (**desmosomes**),
  **tonofibrils**; **Langerhans cells** here. Basale + spinosum =
  **stratum Malpighii**.
- **Stratum granulosum**: flattened cells with **keratohyalin granules**;
  lamellar granules waterproof the skin.
- **Stratum lucidum**: flattened, pale, anucleate cells with **eleidin**.
  **Thick skin only.**
- **Stratum corneum**: **15–20 layers** of dead anucleate cells full of keratin;
  **desquamation**.
- **Thick skin** (palms, soles): thick corneum + **lucidum**, no hair.
  **Thin skin**: no lucidum, hair and sebaceous glands.

## Cells of the Epidermis

- **Melanocyte**: round body with long processes, **between basal cells**; makes
  **eumelanin**. Same number in light and dark skin.
- **Langerhans cell**: like a melanocyte but **no melanin**; mainly in the
  **spinosum**; macrophage-like immune cell.
- **Merkel cell**: keratinocyte-like with **dense granules**; **basal layer**,
  esp. thick skin; with its nerve ending = **Merkel corpuscle**
  (mechanoreceptor).
- **Lymphocytes**: mostly immature **T cells**; with Langerhans cells and
  keratinocytes form **SALT**.

## Dermis and Hypodermis

- **Stratum papillare**: thin, **loose CT**; fibroblasts, macrophages, mast
  cells; dermal papillae; capillaries feeding the epidermis;
  **anchoring fibrils (type VII collagen)**; **Meissner's corpuscles**.
- **Stratum reticulare**: thick, **dense irregular CT**; **type I collagen** and
  **elastic fibers**.
- **Hypodermis**: loose CT with abundant **adipocytes**, highly vascular,
  **Pacinian corpuscles**.

## Sensory Nerve Endings

- **Merkel's disc**: **unencapsulated**; on Merkel cells in the basal epidermis;
  **light touch**.
- **Vater-Pacini**: **oval, onion-like**, concentric lamellae; deep dermis and
  subcutis; **crude touch, pressure, vibration**.
- **Meissner**: **cylindrical**, branched, thin capsule of transverse flattened
  cells; **dermal papillae**; **light touch**.
- **Ruffini**: cylindrical, smaller, **spray-like branches**, thin capsule;
  dermis; **pressure**.
- **Krause**: **round**, **thick capsule**; dermis; **pressure** (practicum;
  older texts: cold).
- **Free nerve endings**: pain, temperature, itch.

## Skin Appendages

- **Hair**: shaft, root (in the follicle), **bulb** (dermal papilla, blood
  vessels, dividing epithelial cells, melanocytes).
- **Hair layers, inside out**: **medulla → cortex → cuticle**.
- **Follicle sheaths**: outer **dermal root sheath** (CT;
  **arrector pili attaches here**) and inner **epidermal root sheath**
  (epithelium), separated by the **glassy membrane**.
- **Arrector pili**: smooth muscle; raises the hair (goosebumps).
- **Sebaceous gland**: simple branched acinar, **holocrine** (sebum), opens
  **into the hair follicle** (pilosebaceous unit); absent from palms/soles.
- **Eccrine sweat gland**: simple coiled tubular, **merocrine**, opens
  **onto the skin surface**; secretory part has dark cells, clear cells and
  **myoepithelial cells**; duct = 2-layered cuboidal.
- **Myoepithelial cells**: in the **secretory portion**, not the duct.
- **Apocrine sweat gland**: larger, **wide lumen**, opens
  **into a hair follicle**; axilla, areola, anal region.
- **Nail**: keratin plate on the dorsal distal phalanx; **eponychium** proximal,
  **hyponychium** distal.
