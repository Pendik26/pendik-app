# Virtual Lab

The Virtual Lab is a practice simulator for the **PhysioEx 9.1 Exercise 2:
Skeletal Muscle Physiology** dry lab. Use it to prepare for the practicum, or
to go over it again afterwards. Its numbers follow the practicum slides
(threshold about 0.8 V, maximal stimulus 8.5 V, optimal length 75 mm), but in
your report always use the values you measure in the real program.

## The seven activities

1. The Muscle Twitch and the Latent Period
2. The Effect of Stimulus Voltage
3. The Effect of Stimulus Frequency
4. Tetanus in Isolated Skeletal Muscle
5. Fatigue in Isolated Skeletal Muscle
6. The Skeletal Muscle Length–Tension Relationship
7. Isotonic Contractions and the Load–Velocity Relationship

Each activity page has the **Background**, the **Steps** to follow, the
equipment, the data table, **What you should find**, and **Check your
understanding** questions with explanations.

## Running an experiment

- Set the controls the activity uses: **Voltage**, **Muscle length**,
  **Stimuli per second** or **Weight**.
- Press **Stimulate** (or **Single Stimulus**, **Multiple Stimulus** and **Stop
  Stimulus** where the activity needs a train of stimuli). The trace is drawn
  on the oscilloscope, slowed down so you can watch it.
- In Activity 1, press **Measure** and move the line (on the trace or with its
  slider) to where the force starts rising: that time is the latent period,
  and it's saved with the row.
- **Record Data** adds the run to the data table. **Clear Tracings** wipes the
  screen; up to eight tracings stay on it, each in its own colour.

## Your data

- The table is kept for each activity on this device, so you can come back to
  it later. **Delete** removes one row; **Clear data** empties the table.
- **Plot Data** draws the graph the activity asks for (for example force
  against voltage).
- **Download CSV** saves the table for a spreadsheet or your report.
