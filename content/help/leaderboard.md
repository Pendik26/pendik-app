# Leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. It shows how your studying compares with other
students, and nobody sees you on it unless you join.

## Joining

Open **Leaderboard** (signed in), choose a **display name** (a nickname is
fine) and press **Join**. You can change the name later, or **Leave
leaderboard** at any time.

Other signed-in students see only your display name, your cohort and your
scores: never your email, your answers or what you got wrong.

## The three boards

- **This week:** points from the last 7 days.
- **All time:** every point since you started.
- **Streak:** your run of study days in a row.

If you've set a cohort, **Who to compare with** switches between **Everyone**
and your cohort.

## How points work

| You | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Answer a quiz or exam question correctly (each question once a day) | 1 |
| Learn a flashcard or figure label (review it on two different days) | 2 |
| Finish an ebook chapter | 10 |
| Each day you study | 5 |

The app tells the server what you did (which card you reviewed, which option
you picked) and the server checks it and does the scoring, with its own key
and its own clock, so points can't be typed in. A short answer you mark
yourself earns nothing. Points never go down: resetting a card or un-ticking
a chapter takes nothing away.

Your studying counts from the day your account started recording it, whether
or not you've joined. Equal scores share a rank.

Days on the leaderboard run on UTC (07:00 to 07:00 WIB), like streaks.
