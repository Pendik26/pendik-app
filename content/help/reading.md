# Ebooks and summaries

## Ebooks

Each subject's ebook is written for this course, chapter by chapter, with
diagrams and figures from the lecture slides.

- **Ebooks** lists every book. Opening one takes you back to the chapter you
  were last reading.
- The contents list sits beside the chapter (on a phone, tap **Contents** at
  the top to open it). Chapters you've finished have a tick.
- At the end of a chapter press **Mark chapter complete**, then use the button
  to go on to the next one. Finished chapters count towards readiness, today's
  plan and the leaderboard (10 points each).
- Some books also list a **PDF** and **Further reading** links in the contents.

## Summaries

A summary is one page per subject: the short version of the whole subject, for
revising. **Summaries** lists them by block. The exam plan's last days often
suggest a summary to skim.

## Reading settings

Above every chapter and summary:

- **A−** and **A+** make the text smaller or larger.
- **Aa** switches to **Atkinson Hyperlegible**, a font designed to be
  easier to read.
- The printer button prints the page, or saves it as a PDF, without the menus
  and buttons.

Your choices apply to every book and summary, and are remembered.

## Links into a section

The [knowledge map](/docs/knowledge-map) and search can open a chapter at one
heading. The heading flashes briefly so you can see where you landed.

## Offline

Ebook chapters and summaries are part of the app, so they work offline once
the app has loaded once.
