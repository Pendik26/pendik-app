# Troubleshooting and FAQ

## My progress is gone

- **Same browser?** As a guest, progress lives in one browser on one device.
  Another browser, a private window or another phone starts empty.
- **Cleared your browser data?** That erases guest progress. Restore it with
  **Progress → Import progress** if you have a backup.
- **Signed in?** Sign in again on the **Account** page: your progress comes
  back from the account.

To never lose progress again, create an account (it syncs) or export a backup
now and then.

## My progress isn't on my other device

Check that you're signed in to the same account on both (the **Account** page
says who you are). Sync runs a few seconds after you study and when you come
back to the app, so open the app on the other device and give it a moment. If
you studied offline, the first device needs to go online once to send it.

## The Google button doesn't work

- Inside Instagram, LINE, TikTok and similar apps, Google blocks sign-in. Open
  the site in Chrome or Safari.
- If you closed the Google window, just press the button again.

## The app looks out of date

If you see **A new version is ready**, press **Refresh**. If not, close every
tab of the app (or the installed app) and open it again.

## A page says "Something went wrong"

Press **Reload** first. If it keeps happening, **Clear local data and reload**
starts the app fresh on this device. That erases guest progress on this
device, so export a backup first if you can. Signed in, your progress comes
back from your account.

## A PDF won't open offline

Each PDF is saved for offline use the first time you open it. Open the ones
you'll need while you're online.

## The Class Drive won't load

- **"Sign in to open the class Drive":** it's for signed-in students.
- **"The class Drive isn't connected":** the site hasn't been set up with a
  Drive folder yet.
- **"Couldn't load the class Drive":** check your connection and try again; if
  you've opened it before, the last copy still shows.

## Alfond doesn't answer

- **Offline:** Alfond needs a connection.
- **"Isn't switched on":** the site hasn't been set up with an AI provider.
- **Allowance used up:** the free daily allowance resets at 07:00 WIB.
- **Slow:** the free AI can take up to a minute to start answering.

## My streak broke, but I studied

Streak days run on UTC: in Indonesia (WIB) a day runs from 07:00 to 07:00. If
you studied after midnight but before 07:00, it counted towards the day before.

## My exam ended by itself

When the timer runs out, the exam is submitted with the answers you gave.
Unanswered questions count as wrong.

## Something in the material is wrong

Tell us: open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/neccrr/medicine/issues) with the subject, where it
is and what's wrong.
