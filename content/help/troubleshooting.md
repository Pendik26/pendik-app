# Troubleshooting and FAQ

## I can't sign in

- **First time?** Your username is your NIM and your first password is
  `pendik26` followed by your NIM. The app then asks you to choose your own.
- **Forgot your password?** Ask an admin to reset it. It goes back to the
  first password and you choose a new one at sign-in.
- **Still can't get in?** Only students on the Pendik 26 roster whose
  account an admin has activated can sign in. Ask an admin to check yours.

## The Google button doesn't work

- Google only works after you've linked it: sign in with your NIM first, then
  link Google from the **Account** page.
- Inside Instagram, LINE, TikTok and similar apps, Google blocks sign-in. Open
  the site in Chrome or Safari.
- If you closed the Google window, just press the button again.

## My progress isn't on my other device

Progress is saved in your account, a moment after you study. Check that
you're signed in with the same NIM on both (the **Account** page says who you
are, and whether everything is saved). Coming back to the app on the other
device loads the latest. If you studied offline, the first device needs to go
online once to save it.

## The Account page says a save failed

Your changes are kept and saved again when the connection is back. Press
**Save now** to try straight away. Don't sign out until it says everything is
saved.

## The app looks out of date

If you see **A new version is ready**, press **Refresh**. If not, close every
tab of the app (or the installed app) and open it again.

## A page says "Something went wrong"

Press **Reload** first. If it keeps happening, reset this device's settings
and reload. That only clears the theme, sidebar and recent pages; your
progress is in your account and isn't affected.

## A PDF won't open offline

Each PDF is saved for offline use the first time you open it. Open the ones
you'll need while you're online.

## The Class Drive is empty or out of date

The Class Drive is copied from the class's Google Drive every morning. A file
added today may only show tomorrow. If it says **Couldn't load the class
Drive**, check your connection and try again.

## Alfond doesn't answer

- **Offline:** Alfond needs a connection.
- **"Isn't switched on":** the site hasn't been set up with an AI provider.
- **Allowance used up:** the daily allowance resets at 07:00 WIB.
- **Slow:** the AI can take up to a minute to start answering.

## My streak broke, but I studied

Streak days run on UTC: in Indonesia (WIB) a day runs from 07:00 to 07:00. If
you studied after midnight but before 07:00, it counted towards the day before.

## My exam ended by itself

When the timer runs out, the exam is submitted with the answers you gave.
Unanswered questions count as wrong. The timer runs on the server, so closing
the tab doesn't pause it.

## Something in the material is wrong

Tell us: open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Pendik26/pendik-app/issues) with the subject,
where it is and what's wrong.
