# Flashcards

Flashcards use **spaced repetition**: a card you know well comes back after
days or weeks, and a card you forget comes back tomorrow. You spend your time
on what you don't know yet.

## A review session

1. Open **Flashcards** and pick a subject. The list shows how many cards are
   due in each.
2. Read the question and answer it in your head.
3. Tap the card (or press <kbd>Space</kbd>) to see the answer.
4. Grade how well you remembered it. The next review date depends on the
   grade.
5. When nothing is left, a summary shows how many you reviewed, recalled and
   missed.

A card you've never seen counts as due, so a new deck starts with every card.

## The grade buttons

| Button | Key | Use it when | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blackout | <kbd>1</kbd> | You had no idea | Back tomorrow, start again |
| Hard | <kbd>2</kbd> | You barely got it, or got it wrong | Back tomorrow, start again |
| Okay | <kbd>3</kbd> | You got it with effort | Next gap grows a little less each time |
| Good | <kbd>4</kbd> | You got it easily | Next gap grows steadily |
| Easy | <kbd>5</kbd> | It was instant | Next gap grows a little more each time |

A card you get right comes back after 1 day, then 6 days, then a longer gap
each time. Graded **Good** every time, the gaps run 1, 6, 15, 38 and 95 days.
A card is **mastered** once its gap reaches 21 days.

Be honest with the grades: grading a guess as Good just pushes a card you
don't know further away.

## Nothing due?

You're caught up. Come back later, or press **Review anyway** to go through the
deck again. Reviewing early doesn't hurt, but the schedule only really needs
you when cards are due.

## Filter by topic

Each card has topic tags. Use **Find a topic** above the card, or tap a tag, to
review only those cards; tap it again (or clear the filter) to go back to the
whole deck. The app remembers your filter for each deck.

## Your hardest cards

Below the session, **Your hardest cards** lists the cards in the deck you've
forgotten most often. They're worth rewriting in your own notes. The
**Progress** page and the exam plan also list the topics you keep forgetting
across every deck.

## Other things on a card

- **Read aloud:** the speaker buttons read the question or the answer out loud
  (your device's voice).
- **Explain this:** after flipping, open it to see the passages of the subject's
  ebook and summary that cover the card. See [Alfond and Explain
  this](/docs/alfond).

## How it fits the rest

Cards you review count towards your streak, today's plan, readiness and the
leaderboard. If you have an account, reviews sync to your other devices.
Exactly how the next date is worked out is in the developer docs.
