# Your data and privacy

## As a guest

Everything you do is stored in this browser on this device, and nowhere else.
Clearing the browser's site data erases it, so keep a backup (**Progress →
Export progress (JSON)**) if it matters to you.

## With an account

Stored in the app's database:

- your name, email, optional cohort and a protected (hashed) password, or your
  Google sign-in;
- your synced progress (see [Accounts and sync](/docs/account) for what
  syncs);
- if you join the leaderboard, your display name and points.

Not stored in your account: the theme, a half-finished quiz, and Alfond's
conversation, which stay on your device.

## Who sees what

- **Nobody** sees your progress except you.
- If you join the [leaderboard](/docs/leaderboard), other signed-in students
  see your display name, cohort and scores. Leave it any time.
- The **class average** on the exam plan is an average only: no one's own
  readiness is shown.
- Questions to **Alfond** and **AI explanations** go through the site's server
  to an AI provider to be answered. Don't put personal details in them.

## The Class Drive

The Class Drive shows only file names and lets you open the files in Google's
viewer. It's for signed-in students and is never shown to search engines.

## Download or delete

On the **Account** page:

- **Download my data** gives you everything in your account as a file.
- **Delete account** removes the account and its progress permanently.

The full [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms) have the
details.
