# Search and shortcuts

## Search

The **Search** page (the magnifier in the sidebar, or the tab bar on a phone)
looks through every flashcard, quiz question, summary section and ebook
chapter at once. It forgives typos, so "mitocondria" still finds
mitochondria.

- Use the **type** chips (Flashcards, Quizzes, Summaries, Ebooks) to show one
  kind of result, and the **subject** chips to narrow to one subject.
- A flashcard or quiz result opens its deck or quiz; a summary or ebook result
  opens that summary or chapter.
- Past-paper exam questions are not searchable, by design.

## The command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac)
anywhere. With nothing typed it offers, in order:

- **Next**: what's left on today's plan;
- **Recent**: the pages you opened last on this device;
- the main pages.

Type part of a page, subject, card or question and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. It
also finds these help pages: try "help streak".

Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere (outside a text box) for the shortcuts of the page
you're on.

## The sidebar

On a computer, the sidebar can shrink to a row of icons to give pages like the
ebook reader more room: use the button at its edge or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> +
<kbd>\\</kbd>. The app remembers your choice.
