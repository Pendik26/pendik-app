# Quizzes

Each subject has a bank of multiple-choice questions, every one with an
explanation. Quizzes are for checking what you know and for finding the gaps.

## Taking a quiz

1. Open **Quizzes** and pick a subject. The list shows your last score and how
   many questions are waiting for a retry.
2. Press **Start quiz**.
3. Answer with a tap, or press <kbd>A</kbd>–<kbd>E</kbd> (or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>5</kbd>).
   You see straight away whether you were right, with the explanation.
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> to go on. The numbered navigator
   and <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> move between questions.
5. At the end you get your score, a full review of every question, and your
   score trend over recent attempts.

Some questions have a figure (a micrograph or a labelled diagram). Tap it to
see it full screen.

If you leave halfway, **Resume** picks up where you left off next time;
**Start over** throws that attempt away.

## Big banks come in sections

A bank of more than 50 questions is split into sections of about 25. Pick one
section at a time below **Start quiz**, or start the whole bank if you have the
time.

## Why the order changes

Questions and their options are shuffled on every attempt. That way you learn
the answer, not its position ("it was C last time").

## Questions you got wrong come back

- **Review missed only** (after a quiz) retries just the questions you missed
  in that attempt.
- Every missed question also joins a **due** list for that subject. **Review
  due questions** asks only those, and a question leaves the list once you
  answer it correctly in a later attempt.
- The [exam plan](/docs/exam-plan) and [Progress](/docs/progress) pages can
  start a **drill** of the questions you miss most often.

## Explain this

After answering, **Explain this** shows the passages from the subject's ebook
and summary that match the question. Signed in, you can also ask the AI to
explain the answer and why your choice was wrong. See [Alfond and Explain
this](/docs/alfond).

## How scores are counted

Your score is the questions you got right out of the questions in that
attempt; an unanswered question counts as wrong. Quiz scores feed into
readiness on the exam plan and into the leaderboard (1 point per correct
answer).
