# Timed exams

The **Exam** section has timed practice exams for each block. They feel like
the real thing: one clock for the whole paper, and (if you choose) no feedback
until you hand it in.

## Choosing an exam

1. Open **Exam** and pick your block.
2. If the block has several **exam packages** (for example past papers from
   different years or sources), pick one. A block without packages gets an
   exam drawn from its subjects' quiz banks.
3. Choose a mode:
   - **Real exam mode:** no feedback until you submit, like the real thing.
   - **Instant feedback mode:** see the correct answer and explanation after
     each question.
4. Press **Start exam**.

An exam has up to 100 questions and gives you **1 minute per question**, so a
64-question paper runs 64 minutes.

## During the exam

- Answer with a tap or <kbd>A</kbd>–<kbd>E</kbd> (<kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>5</kbd>),
  and move with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> or the navigator.
- **Flag for review** marks a question to come back to. The navigator shows
  which questions are answered and which are flagged.
- The timer turns red in the last 2 minutes. When time runs out, the exam is
  submitted as it stands.
- **Submit exam now** hands it in early. If questions are unanswered or
  flagged, you're asked to confirm first.

An exam isn't saved part-way, so sit it in one go.

## Afterwards

- Your score, with every question explained in the full review.
- **Retake missed only** runs a short timed round of just the ones you got
  wrong.
- Each package keeps a score history, and your mock scores appear on the [exam
  plan](/docs/exam-plan), where they count towards readiness and the projected
  exam score.

## About the past papers

Past-paper questions are only for practice inside the app. They aren't
searchable and never appear on public pages.
