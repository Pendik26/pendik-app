# Exam plan and today's plan

The **Exam plan** turns "the exam is in three weeks" into what to do today. It
is built from your own progress, so it changes as you study.

## Set it up

Open **Exam plan** and pick your block. Under **Settings**:

- **Exam date:** the block's official date is filled in when there is one. You
  can set your own instead (it syncs with your account).
- **Study time a day:** how many minutes you have; 60 to start with. Today's
  plan is sized to fit it.
- **Target score:** the mock-exam score you're aiming for; 70% to start with.
- **Usual study time:** used only for the calendar file.

**Add to calendar** downloads a calendar file with your study sessions and the
exam. **Plan my week with Alfond** asks the [study assistant](/docs/alfond) to
turn the plan into a week (signed in).

## Today's plan

A checklist of what to do today. **Home** leads with its next item and a
**Start** button, and the end of every study session offers the one after
(**Up next**). Items tick themselves off as you study; you don't have to tick
anything.

What goes in, roughly in this order:

1. Cards and figure labels that are **due**. These always get a place.
2. Close to the exam, a timed **mock exam**.
3. Questions you **missed** before, to retry.
4. From two weeks out, your **weakest** topics and questions.
5. Until the last few days, **new** cards and labels, an unread chapter and a
   short quiz.

New material is spread so you see all of it before the next phase. Miss a day
and the rest is spread over the days left; nothing piles up on one day.

## Phases

| Days to the exam | Phase | The plan focuses on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 or more | Learn | new material, reading, quizzes |
| 4 to 14 | Strengthen | your weak spots |
| 2 to 3 | Mock exams | timed mocks and reviews |
| 1 | Final review | due reviews and the weakest subject's summary |
| 0 | Exam day | a summary or two to skim |

## Readiness

**Readiness** (0–100) is how prepared you are for each subject, from:

- how well you know its **flashcards** and **figure labels**,
- your **quiz** accuracy, and how much of the bank you've covered,
- how many **chapters** you've finished.

A part the subject doesn't have is left out. A perfect score on one small
section doesn't count as knowing the whole bank: coverage matters.

The block's readiness is the average of its subjects. Once you've sat **mock
exams**, they count for 30% of it.

## Mock exams and the projection

Mock exams are the timed exams from the [Exam section](/docs/exams). After two
mocks on different days, the plan draws your trend and projects a score for
exam day, against your target. **Sit one** starts the next one.

## Weak spots and your class

- **Weak spots** lists the topics you keep forgetting, the questions you miss
  most and the labels that keep slipping, each with a one-tap drill.
- **Your class** (signed in) shows your readiness against your cohort's
  average, once at least 3 classmates have one.

The exact formulas are in the developer docs, if you want to check a number.
