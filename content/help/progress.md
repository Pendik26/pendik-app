# Progress, streaks and backups

The **Progress** page is everything you've done, in one place.

## What's on it

- **Your block:** readiness, the countdown to the exam and your weakest
  subject, then your current streak, longest streak and study days.
- **Subjects:** a meter each for cards, labels, quiz, reading and mock exams.
- **Trends:** this week against last week, cards and labels reviewed each day,
  and your quiz and mock-exam scores over time.
- **Activity:** a heatmap of the last 16 weeks, darker the more you studied.
  Tap a day to see what you did. Once there's enough history, it also shows
  the time of day you study most.
- **Weak spots:** the topics you keep forgetting, the questions you miss most
  and the labels that keep slipping, with drills.
- **Milestones** you've earned, and the next one to aim for.
- **Ask Alfond about my progress** (signed in).

## Streaks

A day counts towards your streak when you study anything: review cards or
labels, take a quiz, read a chapter or sit an exam. Your **current
streak** counts back from today, or from yesterday if you haven't studied yet
today, so it doesn't break until the day is over.

> Streak days follow UTC, which is 07:00 to 07:00 in Indonesia (WIB). Studying
> at 01:00 counts towards the day before.

## Backing up (guests)

As a guest, your progress is only in this browser. To keep a copy:

- **Export progress (JSON)** downloads a file with all of it.
- **Import progress** loads that file, on this or another device. It replaces
  the progress it contains on that device, so import before you start studying
  there, not after.

If you haven't backed up for 14 days, a reminder appears. With an account you
don't need to: your progress syncs automatically, and a backup file is just an
extra copy.
